## Educational Research Notice

MANU AI is an **educational research project** for Family Digital Safety & Parental Control.
This application teaches users about:
- Cybersecurity awareness and threat detection
- App privacy auditing and permission analysis
- Protection against phishing, insecure networks, and digital fraud
- Parental control and family safety protocols

**All features are defensive (white-hat) only.** No surveillance, tracking, or unauthorized access capabilities are included.

---

## Hardware Requirements

- ARM64 Android device (arm64-v8a)
- 4GB+ free storage
- Android 7.0+ (API 24)
- Microphone (for voice features)
- Internet connection (for AI chat)

---

## Software Requirements

- Termux (from F-Droid, NOT Play Store)
- OpenJDK 17
- Node.js 18+
- Android SDK (cmdline-tools)
- NDK r26b (with ARM64 symlink workaround)
- Gradle 8.2+
